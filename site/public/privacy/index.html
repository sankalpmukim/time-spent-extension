Time Spent — Privacy Policy

Effective: November 19, 2026

The short version

Time Spent works entirely locally. Until you explicitly sign in to sync, nothing ever leaves your browser. If you sign in, the only data uploaded is what's listed below, and it's used for exactly one thing: merging your totals across your own browsers and devices.

What is stored locally

  • Which websites you chose to track (hostnames, e.g. reddit.com).
  • Seconds spent per tracked website per day, in your browser's local extension storage.
  • Your display thresholds and overlay position.
  • Local history is pruned after 90 days.

What is uploaded if you enable sync (opt-in)

  • Your Google account email, name, and Google account ID — used only to identify your sync account.
  • Per-day, per-site time totals (e.g. reddit.com: 3,742 s on 2026-11-19) — merged across devices by keeping the larger value.

Sync is turned off unless you sign in. Signing out stops all uploads. Deleting the extension removes all local data; contact time-spent@mukim.dev to delete server-side sync data.

What we never do

  • No page content, URLs, browsing history, keystrokes, or form data are ever read or uploaded.
  • No analytics, telemetry, or third-party trackers in the extension.
  • No selling or sharing of data. Ever.

Sign-in details

Sign-in uses Google Identity Services via a relay page hosted on Cloudflare (time-spent-site.sankalpmukim.workers.dev). The Google ID token is verified by the sync backend (Convex, watchful-salamander-923.convex.cloud); sessions are opaque tokens stored hashed. Passwords are never handled — there are none.
