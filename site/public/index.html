Time Spent

A shame counter for your doomscrolling — in big digits you can't unsee.

Install for Firefox Install for Chrome

How it works

  • Off by default. You pick the sites (youtube.com, reddit.com, x.com…) and only those get a counter.
  • A big timer sits in the corner of the page all day, counting up while the tab is visible and focused. Switch away and it stops.
  • After your first threshold, the digits grow — linearly, the more you scroll.
  • After your second threshold, they turn red and pulse.
  • Drag the counter to any corner if it's covering something. That's the only control you get. There is no hide button. That's the point.

Local-first, sync optional

No account needed

Everything is stored in your browser. Sign in only if you want your totals merged across Firefox, Chrome, and Firefox on Android.

Anti-cheat merge

When syncing, devices merge daily totals by always keeping the larger number. Clearing one browser doesn't reset anything.

Minimal permissions

No access to any site until you explicitly track it. Time counting happens locally, per second, only while you're actually looking.

Browsers

  • Firefox desktop + Firefox on Android (Manifest V2)
  • Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc and other Chromium browsers (Manifest V3)
Time Spent is free and open source. Sync is powered by a tiny Convex backend; the sign-in relay runs on Cloudflare. Questions? time-spent@mukim.dev
